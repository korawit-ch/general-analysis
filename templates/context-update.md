# Context update

Use when a project revision, requirement, or decision changes what later work can rely on.

## Trigger and evidence

- **Trigger** — [Landed change, accepted requirement or decision, comparison change, or lost evidence]
- **Prior sources** — [Last verified project commit and relevant task or decision version]
- **Current sources** — [Candidate or landed revision, updated requirement, confirming owner and link]
- **Coverage** — [Areas affected, access or verification gaps]

## Affected artifacts

Repeat for each material change. Link the owning artifact.

- **Artifact and claim** — [File or record and the behavior, decision, or assumption it states]
- **Impact** — [What changed and downstream plans, tests, docs, or reviews affected]
- **Verification** — [Code trace or check, revision, and result]
- **Update state** — [Current, pending, or unverified, with last verified point, owner and next action]

## Closeout

- **Decisions** — [Accepted changes, linked prior decisions and unresolved questions]
- **Changes applied** — [Owning PR or doc revision and links checked]
- **Remaining work** — [Pending item, exact next action, responsible owner, expected trigger]
