# Change brief

Use the owning task or PR when the brief fits there. Link a versioned plan when the change requires coordination or rollout evidence.

## Intake

- **Task source** — [Provider and project, stable ID and link, version or updated time, date read]
- **Project source** — [Repository, target branch or commit, relevant instructions and project context]
- **Scope and success** — [Requested behavior, actor, observable result, exclusions, existing work]
- **Decisions and dependencies** — [Accepted links, unresolved conflicts, owner, impact on the plan]

## Reconciliation and acceptance

Repeat for each material criterion.

- **Criterion** — [Stable ID, source passage, accepted or proposed status]
- **Behavior** — [Preconditions → action → expected outcome, including relevant failure or data guarantees]
- **Relationship** — [Covered, extends, new, owned elsewhere, or conflict. Link current implementation and owner.]
- **Proof** — [Planned or actual path and check, tested revision and result when available]

## Plan and handoff

- **Current flow** — [Entry point, existing owner and reusable paths, enforcement and effects]
- **Changes and sequence** — [Deliverables, files or layers, dependencies, rollout and compatibility as needed]
- **Impact and checks** — [Consumers, failure paths, commands, planned or actual state]
- **Open work** — [Pending decision or check, consequence, owner and next action]
- **Context** — [Affected artifact or decision, source PR, who refreshes it and when]
